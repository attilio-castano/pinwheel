# Research results

This index records completed conclusions and what could justify reopening them.
Detailed studies own measurements, assumptions, commands, and proof boundaries.
[Status](status.md) alone owns the active allocation; [journal](journal.md) provides
historical identities. Updated 2026-09-17 to combine the hardware correspondence
closure and cache-enable screen with the UART receive and continuous-clock results,
the flow-correlation diagnosis and the pin-sampler comparison.

| [Physical flow correlation](../physical-correlation-study.md) | The resizer finished at +0.055 ns on estimates while extraction reported −5.049 ns; fitted extracted wire capacitance is 1.5–2.1× the LEF values (R² 0.986). | [Pin sampler](../pin-sampler-study.md) | A structural two-register `incoming` pipeline refines the reference machine on the delayed pin history (pair traces; registered outputs unaffected); UART `Safe` bounds survive the uniform delay. Emitted inner RTL is byte-identical to the read-back-proved control; sampled RTL passes reference equivalence, wrong-depth rejections and the pin-shifted independent oracle. Against a matched control at −0.797 ns (worst path per endpoint from `incoming`; register-launched paths also −0.294 ns), the candidate routes at **+0.090 ns** slow setup with every launch family met, no hold violations and a passing implemented-netlist regression. Combined with width-8 clock gating it keeps the area saving (608,058 µm², 67.4%) and hold margin but misses slow setup by 2.251 ns, 2.054 ns on register-launched paths alone, all ending at the cached-word clock gate. Retain both as experimental variants. | One run, no margin against a 0.6 ns spread between equivalent RTLs; electrical-limit violations remain; layout checks not run; global overflow was left to the detailed router. No direct Lean read-back of the sampled RTL; no metastability, wrapper or board claim. SPI and I²C behind the added two edges are analysed in the input-latency row. The combined run's extra 1.8 ns of path delay is unattributed; an ungated cached word or the cache-enable variant is the untested remedy. |
| [Structural timing](../structural-timing.md) | Lean arrival levels with composition, wrapper and unreachable-implies-independent theorems; pin isolation holds for every inner netlist. The report reproduces 13 MLIR-measured depths exactly, agrees with twelve mapped cone depths (r = 0.954, all eight candidate changes in the same direction) and with the routed control's port-family ranking, and counts 6,172 recirculating bits against about 6,200 hold-violating endpoints. It locates 41 of the successor loop's 101 gate levels after the lookup and shows the cached word's enable arriving 49 levels after its data. Adopt as the first screen for structural questions. | Ordinal only: no fanout, buffering, wire or placement. A fanout-aware cost is suggested, not built; the other three suggested abstractions (enables, latency, memory) are built. |
| [Register enables](../register-enables.md) | All 581 storing registers of the proved bodies are, by `rfl`, `mux enable data hold` (index entries under a slice); Lean certifies each view against the emitted next-state without changing any expression, and gating policies are definitions with theorems (only certified registers; never the cached word). Storage enables arrive at level 23–29 versus 99 for the cached word. Applied to the read-back-proved RTL, the plans match the gated netlists bit by bit, pass the oracle with every storage register observed and reject all stuck-enable mutants (1,160/1,160); mapped area −9.0% (66 gates) and −16.2% (580 gates), delay unchanged. Adopt as the way to choose gating. | That a clock gate implements the enable contract is simulation and mutation evidence, not a proof. Hold at directly fed gated flip-flops, 580 gated branches in clock-tree synthesis, routability and the predicted hold-repair saving are unmeasured; one routed confirmation needs its own allocation. |
| [Input latency](../input-latency.md) | Latency is a parameter of the pin-level contracts, bridged to the structural pipeline. UART: bounds shift by the pipeline depth. SPI: `d + tco ≤ halfCycles` suffices, proved for reference and compiled program and tight in execution. I²C: for every target, the formerly guarded STOP hold reported a false `busFault` for `d ≥ 1`, each clock rise spends `d` wait-budget units, and the high phase starts prematurely when `d > phaseCycles`; ACK sampling is unaffected. The STOP echo is removed by qualifying bus-free time after STOP in both reference controllers; the explicit, counted and register-read programs use the existing `qualify` instruction there, with the correspondence theorems re-proved and no ISA or RTL change. Closed-loop executions of references and compiled programs succeed for `d ≤ phaseCycles`, `d < waitCycles`. The pin-sampled RTL, byte-identical to the routed candidate, replays eight closed-loop writes and register reads behind its two registers; the former guarded record faults there. | No closed-loop I²C theorem over all targets. The rate conditions are obligations on the configuration; the compiler does not check them against a declared latency. For `d = 0`, a line held low after STOP now yields `timeout`, not `busFault`. SPI capture compensation is not built. Digital model only. |
| [Memory abstraction](../memory-abstraction.md) | A memory contract with read latency, one write and `p` read ports; flip-flops refine latency 0 and registered ports latency 1, proved once for every size; the loader image, its cursor-decoded writes, its two-port fetch and the routed backend's dense words are instances, with no expression changed. The prefetch machine runs the reference against a latency-one memory — both candidate successors read from next-state values on two ports — and refines the atomic reference edge for edge; commit refills the fetch and initialization owes nothing. The decoupled machine takes the addresses from the dispatch decision and the target, with a start word loaded on commit; its structural netlist on the general backend brings the deepest register endpoint from 101 gate levels to 63 and the cached word from 101 to 38, proved to refine the reference and passing the independent oracle on the emitted RTL. 5,161 closed-loop edges, 24 taken branches, for both machines. Fetch organizations are a parameter: a policy with `p` read ports and three obligations, the refinement of the reference proved once under an optional rule on inputs, and a backend parametric in the policy, so that a structural backend is the policy's fed word, two wires and its registers' updates. The decoupled (three-port), two-port and one-port organizations are instances with proved, emitted and mapped backends: +19.4%, +12.8% and +5.3% area over the sampled candidate, all with less combinational delay and a deepest register endpoint of 63–65 gate levels against 101; re-basing the existing backends left their RTL byte-identical. The one-port rule (no branching `checked` record with a zero duration) can be assumed, enforced by a push filter like the capacity check, and is proved of the compilers: I²C write and read ready whenever a phase lasts two cycles, UART and SPI transmission always, the UART receiver never. Routed once behind the sampler under the clock-gating overlay, the one-port backend fits (69.2% utilization, zero routing violations, hold met) and moves slow setup from −2.251 ns for the same-overlay control to −0.187 ns, the register-launched family from −2.054 ns to −0.053 ns; the limiting family is now the loader's data port through the capacity check on the command decode, and the worst register path ends where the level model ranks deepest. With the command-split form applied once in the generic backend — every policy backend inherits it, and `Storage/DataPort.lean` proves that no register of the fetch path has a structural path from the data port, so its next value does not depend on the presented word — the routed repeat (`oneport-split-01`) meets setup at all three extracted corners with clock gating: slow +0.602 ns with no violating endpoint, hold met, zero routing violations, DRC, LVS and antenna clean, flow exit 0; the data port's worst path ends at a loader output (+4.383 ns), as the level model and the theorem state. The two-port backend, routed once under the same contract, did not finish: 75.2% utilization at global routing, 2 h of one-clock-net-per-round congestion repair, and the wall-time limit before detailed routing; on this test rectangle routability ends between 69% and 75% utilization. Routed under the sampled candidate's contract, neither of two attempts of the decoupled backend reached timing: placement failed at 81% utilization, then global routing did not converge at 80.7% (control 66.9%); the level gain has no routed confirmation, and at the diagnostic floorplan area binds before depth. | The structural backend is flip-flop storage with fetched registers, not a macro; no macro timing claim. Levels are ordinal. The composite read is latency one only if the map or the dictionary stays combinational; two read ports or two copies are required, or one port under the per-program rule. The one-port RTL regression uses ready-mode vectors and the readiness filter is in no emitted netlist; the one-port organization has two routed samples (−0.187 ns before the split, +0.602 ns after), one run each, and the register family's 1.15 ns gain between them is larger than its one-level change in depth explains, cause not isolated; the two-port backend has no extracted timing; the theory is edge-for-edge (no stalls) and latency one. Read enables, masks and write-first collisions are outside the contract; latches keep their write-phase obligation. |
| [The whole chip in Lean](../whole-chip.md) | The chip is one Lean netlist with Tiny Tapeout's ports: pin map, two-register samplers on every input, a three-pin serial loader (72-bit frames), any proved core, an output map. A layer in front of a netlist is a `Feeder`, with one theorem for what the inner netlist then does, for every layer and every core. The receiver is proved equal to its functions; any host session delivers exactly its commands, in order, among quiet edges; no pin has a combinational path past one flip-flop; with a fetch-policy backend the pins show the atomic reference machine on the fed history. The upload theorem closes the chain from pins to programs: begin, 322 words and commit, however spaced, leave those words as the active image with the engine stopped; a program's stream makes the bank read as that program and passes validation and (at most 32 distinct records) the capacity check; a held program is the one the engine runs, edge for edge; `chip_runs_upload` states it from the serial pins for both chips. Mapped, the whole one-port chip is +0.8% over the test-boundary core. | The emitted `tt_um_pinwheel` has no simulation against an independent serial driver, no gate equivalence and no placed or routed run. The official outline is 6×4 since 2026-09-18, the die area of every routed run here; its pin template has not been applied yet, and on it the one-port chip is the candidate (the two-port core did not route at 75.2%). Digital samples only: metastability, pin-to-pin skew and board timing are outside the theorems; status read-back over the serial pins does not exist; the one-port statement needs ready programs, so the UART receiver as compiled is outside it. |
