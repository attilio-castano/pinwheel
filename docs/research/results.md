## Physical organization, repairs, and qualification

The [SRAM continuation](../physical/sram-extraction-results.md) qualifies the
351-pin spelling adapter and chip boundary comparison, including native signal
and power fault rejection. Every macro shape and text label is preserved in GDS.
The [comparison policy](../physical/sram-comparison-results.md) qualifies all four
unchanged context fixtures in both extraction modes, with 42 defect rejections.
It corrects six earlier database matches that failed their final native port
check and independently demonstrates the disconnected-output false positive
when interface safeguards are removed. Internal Magic extraction and complete
SRAM LVS remain unqualified. The subsequent
[hierarchical integration](../physical/sram-integration-results.md) preserves
ports and array structure, then reproduces eight remaining circuit-type
failures. The [tile diagnostic](../physical/sram-tile-results.md) isolates a
0.260-versus-0.200 µm resistor-width disagreement. Only its explicitly modified
diagnostic passes; the supplied tile remains unqualified.
