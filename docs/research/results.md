# Research results

This index records completed conclusions and what could justify reopening them.
Detailed studies own measurements, assumptions, commands, and proof boundaries.
[Status](status.md) alone owns the active allocation; [journal](journal.md) provides
historical identities. Updated 2026-09-17 to combine the hardware correspondence
closure and cache-enable screen with the UART receive and continuous-clock results,
the flow-correlation diagnosis and the pin-sampler comparison.

| Study / evidence owner | Conclusion and disposition | Reopening condition or remaining obligation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [UART/SPI shared engine](../engine-model.md), [original complete core](../core-hardware.md) | Timed actions and precise capture support both protocols on one reloadable machine, with compiler/core proofs and independent RTL checks. Retain as the original baseline. | New protocol needs must be expressed in the semantics and validated; this original core alone does not cover reactive I²C. |
| [One-byte UART receive](../uart-receive.md) | Digital 8N1 receive, false-start rejection at midpoint, and framing-error results compile to existing reactive instructions. All 13,298 period/input configurations fit 256 addresses and 32 dictionary records; mixed-protocol structural and four-backend RTL checks pass. | Asynchronous input synchronization, concurrent TX/RX, and serial program loading need their own contracts and evidence. Continuous supervision is recorded separately below; its new buffer has no circuit implementation yet. Physical operating frequency remains unqualified. |
| [UART link timing](../uart-link.md) | Numerical clock and bounded observation-age conditions imply start detection, all samples, and one-byte roundtrip through both compilers. Ideal reception covers all shared periods 8–256; executable checks cover 3,872 frames and 1,136 safe timing combinations. | A physical sampler must justify the digital age bound. Single-frame bounds alone do not guarantee time to rearm between frames. Sufficient bounds are not claimed optimal. |
| [Continuous UART receive](../uart-stream.md) | A one-entry result buffer has proved occurrence ordering, loss accounting, reset flushing, and independence from reception timing. Finite ideal back-to-back frames at every shared period 8–256 compose through the existing compiled RX and a Lean supervisor. All 65,536 ordered byte pairs and 11,844,449 RX edges pass independent wire/queue checks. | Unequal-clock reception is handled by the subsequent continuous clock contract. The supervisor/buffer circuit, physical sampler, loader composition, and concurrent TX/RX scheduling require further implementation and evidence. |
| [Continuous reception with unequal clocks](../uart-stream-clocks.md) | Two additional RX ticks in the sufficient bound reserve rearm and idle-high observation. Lean proves finite-stream completion pulses under unequal constant clocks and varying bounded observation age, including the compiled RX supervisor. All ideal shared periods 8–256 remain covered. The 24-suite gate passes; the new suite checks 392 streams, 38,808 frames, 10,531,120 RX edges, and 13,056 candidate timing windows. | The bound is sufficient, not optimal. Physical sampling must justify the age envelope; clock-frequency jitter, repeated TX launch scheduling, supervisor/buffer circuitry, atomic loader composition, and concurrent TX/RX remain separate obligations. |
| [Reactive I²C](../compiled-i2c.md), [register reads](../i2c-register-read.md) | Waits, drive/release, guarded timing, and conditional branches support the bounded write/read models on the shared engine. | Broader I²C behavior, synchronization, analog effects, and physical compliance need their own assumptions and evidence. |
| [Counted byte loops](../looped-i2c.md), [binary images](../binary-images.md) | Reusable templates preserve the modeled cycles and reduce the example load image from 715 to 205 bytes. | Serialized bytes do not determine hardware cost; distinguish load-time expansion from runtime repetition. |
| [E64 frontends](../execution-hardware.md), [integrated core](../reactive-core-hardware.md) | Indexed stores save generic storage/cells at the cost of a longer lookup dependency. Retain direct and indexed evidence. | Compare complete implementations under matched capacity and timing, not isolated memory counts. |
| [Atomic loader](../atomic-loader.md), [technology mapping](../technology-mapping.md) | Atomic staging/commit is proved; the 64-entry double-bank mapped reference is too large for the nominal allocation before routing. | A different storage organization or capacity contract must retain replacement semantics and include the complete machine cost. |
| [General storage candidate](../storage-study.md) | A 32-entry dictionary, 55-bit records, and a current-word cache reduce typical mapped cell area from 1.055 to 0.562 mm². Selected for physical investigation. | Capacity remains explicit; emitter equivalence and physical feasibility do not follow from component proofs or area sums. |
| [Bounded runtime repetition](../storage-study.md#bounded-runtime-repetition) | A two-byte I²C prototype maps to 0.192 mm², with narrower scope and a longer slow mapping estimate than the general combined candidate. Retain as research. | Generalize the accepted programs and establish complete integration/timing evidence before treating it as a replacement. |
| [SRAM/latch review](../storage-primitives.md) | Concrete primitives exist, but their latency and write behavior differ from the current combinational reader. Deferred. | Prove successor availability for continuous one-cycle branches and image replacement; measure the complete wrapper and implementation. |
| [Timed components and interfaces](../timed-components.md) | Exact-edge refinements and checked signal interfaces make replacement obligations explicit; the completed refactor preserved baseline emitted bytes. Adopted within the documented scope. | Extend abstractions when actual callers need them; no full-library migration or emitter proof is implied. |
| [Initial physical baseline](../physical-validation.md) | The diagnostic 6×4 core routes and passes recorded layout/netlist checks, but slow setup misses by 6.254 ns and electrical limits remain. Keep as a failed timing baseline. | Require final extracted timing/electrical closure and official wrapper/floorplan integration before fit or frequency claims. |
| [F1/F2 flow controls](../successor-fetch-study.md) | F1 leaves worst setup essentially unchanged; F2 reduces the miss to 5.055 ns with less hold margin and more slew violations. Neither qualifies a default. | A further change must improve complete closure under the same comparison boundary, not one metric alone. |
| [Late-index / late-record screens](../successor-fetch-study.md#architectural-screen) | Both preserve structural behavior and pass RTL regression. Late record is larger/slower than late index in both mapped corners. Late index costs about 4.8% area without measured slow-corner gain. Neither routed. | A new bottleneck diagnosis or materially improved candidate must justify physical expense; the screen does not prove routing could never help. |
| [Targeted source-family STA](../successor-fetch-study.md#targeted-launch-family-timing) | F2 protocol-input slack is −4.928 ns, close to loader-data slack of −5.055 ns. The earlier −1.299 ns protocol row was not the family worst. | Decoder cleanup alone cannot establish closure; compare both families and retain the same constraints. |
| [Command-decoder isolation](../successor-fetch-study.md#command-decoder-experiment) | The structural theorem, mapped dependency removal and independent regressions pass. The matched physical follow-up below replaces the earlier pending-routing disposition. | Keep screen and physical identities separate; no default promotion follows from connectivity alone. |
| [Countdown artifact slice](../hardware-closure.md#countdown-artifact-interpretation-rather-than-a-compiler-proof) | Actual emitted RTL read-back has kernel-checked transition/trace equality; all 19 RTL/generic-gate points pass, with corrupted RTL and invalid import shapes rejected. Adopt this restricted artifact-validation method. | Trusted Yosys frontend/JSON interpretation and equivalence engine remain explicit. No universal emitter proof or technology/physical claim. |
| [Composed dense cached backend](../hardware-closure.md#full-backend-rtl-read-back) | Lean checks all 607 registers and 33 outputs of the emitted RTL against the typed netlist and its initialized reference trace theorem. Old/new RTL and generic gates pass all equivalence points; independent storage checks and two mappings pass. | Verilog/process and JSON interpretation remain trusted. Technology-mapped sequential equivalence, external integration and physical closure remain open; the legacy emitter remains the default. |
| [External pin contract](../external-interface.md) | The proposed two-register sampling pipeline has an explicit two-edge digital latency theorem; open-drain commands refine the ideal bus interpretation. | Implement the transport/wrapper against actual pin, reset, clock-domain and electrical constraints; compose the added latency with protocol bounds. |
| [Matched command-split routing](../successor-fetch-study.md#matched-command-split-physical-comparison) | Final extracted setup still misses by 5.049 ns versus F2's 5.055 ns; area falls about 0.91%, but setup violations rise and hold margin shrinks. Implemented-netlist regression passes. The one-hour attempt stops during Magic DRC; DRC/LVS completion is open. Do not promote. | Diagnose the measured loader-cursor-to-cache path before another screen/run. Completing remaining layout checks would not repair the measured timing/electrical failure. |
| [Program-bank selection](../bank-selection-study.md) | Complete Lean refinements and emitted-RTL read-backs pass for the composed command-split control and late bank-selection candidate. Cursor logic depth falls 32 → 25, but protocol depth grows 35 → 38; about 2.1% more area buys only 0.15% better slow ABC delay and worsens typical delay 6.6%. Retain; no routing or default promotion. | A useful full-core slow-corner gain must justify area/physical expense. The separate cache-enable follow-up below evaluates the next hypothesis on the smaller control. |
| [Cache-update enable](../cache-enable-study.md) | Exact register updates, initialized reference traces and both emitted RTLs pass Lean checks with standard axioms; equivalence, independent regressions and corruptions pass. Typical/slow area falls 0.38% / 0.45% and ABC delay falls 9.11% / 1.31%. Cursor/protocol logic depths improve to 28 / 32. Retain as an experimental variant; no physical run or default promotion. | Direct control dependencies leave the enable, but an indirect cursor path remains through the shared read. A fresh composed control/candidate physical comparison must measure whether the modest slow gain survives routing; timing/electrical closure remains open. |
| [Physical flow correlation](../physical-correlation-study.md) | The resizer finished at +0.055 ns on estimates while extraction reported −5.049 ns; fitted extracted wire capacitance is 1.5–2.1× the LEF values (R² 0.986). Calibrated `LAYERS_RC` moves the same RTL to −0.153 ns with 56 violating endpoints and no slew/capacitance violations. Adopt calibrated estimates as the physical comparison baseline. Clock-gated storage saves 18.5% cell area after hold repair but two attempts fail global routing on Metal3. | One run per configuration; nominal extraction only. Clock gating needs a routable configuration, gate equivalence and an implemented-netlist regression. Horizontal routability of the official outline is unmeasured. |
| [Pin sampler](../pin-sampler-study.md) | A structural two-register `incoming` pipeline refines the reference machine on the delayed pin history (pair traces; registered outputs unaffected); UART `Safe` bounds survive the uniform delay. Emitted inner RTL is byte-identical to the read-back-proved control; sampled RTL passes reference equivalence, wrong-depth rejections and the pin-shifted independent oracle. Against a matched control at −0.797 ns whose 67 violating paths all launch from `incoming`, the candidate routes at **+0.090 ns** slow setup with no setup/hold violations and a passing implemented-netlist regression. Retain as an experimental variant. | One run, no margin against a 0.6 ns spread between equivalent RTLs; electrical-limit violations remain; layout checks not run; global overflow was left to the detailed router. No direct Lean read-back of the sampled RTL; no metastability, wrapper or board claim. I²C timeout/stretch contracts are not yet rechecked against the added two edges. |
