## Contract and main uncertainty

declares 117 register fields / 3,151 bits; the frontend declares thirteen fields
/ 389 bits. Existing optimized frontend evidence retains 370 physical FFs. Raw
state equality cannot be assumed across that quotient: preserve raw state or
prove an explicit cold-established and preserved projection for constants,
aliases and omitted coordinates.

Source graph hints, cut suggestions and solver results are untrusted. Lean
must reconstruct their connection to the actual typed expressions and imported
RTL equations. Yosys parsing/process normalization and the restricted importer
remain explicit interpretation boundaries. Digital SRAM contracts do not
qualify physical macros or electrical timing. Fresh artifacts do not inherit
historical candidate identity from an old manifest.
